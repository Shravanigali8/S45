Clean Energy Technology Reports

Technology Readiness Analysis

This chart presents a comparative analysis of technological maturity across various methods of repurposing coal and gas plants into clean energy hubs. Technologies tracked include solar PV integration, battery storage, clean hydrogen production, small modular nuclear reactors, and carbon capture. The readiness levels reflect the global adoption and development stage of each technology, based on nearly 170 repowering projects tracked worldwide.

Data source: Carnegie Endowment – Carbon to Clean Tracker

Technical Implementation

The Reports page visualizes clean energy repowering technologies using a line chart powered by Chart.js. Data is retrieved asynchronously from our Express backend API, which is protected using JWT-based authentication. The Angular component manages data flow and rendering, ensuring responsive behavior across all screen sizes.
