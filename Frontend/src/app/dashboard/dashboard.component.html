Technical Details of S45

The S45 application offers secure user registration and login using JWT authentication, password hashing, and salting. Built with Angular, Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, and CORS for handling cross-origin requests, it ensures robust security and efficient data retrieval on innovations by region and technology.

Summary - Transforming Fossil Fuel Power Plants into Clean Energy Hubs

A global trend is emerging where aging coal and gas power stations are being converted into clean energy hubs. Instead of merely retiring these plants, their infrastructure is being repurposed to deploy renewables, batteries, nuclear reactors, low-carbon fuels, and more. This approach offers a glimpse into how transformative change can be enacted in the energy sector.

The Carbon to Clean Tracker catalogs these repurposed—or repowered—sites, featuring nearly 170 projects primarily in developed countries, with significant opportunities in emerging markets as well. In the United States and Australia, the focus is on battery storage and renewable energy. European projects showcase a diversified mix of clean power and alternative fuels, while Asian sites primarily utilize biofuels and hydrogen products.

In some instances, coal plant sites are being used to balance the power grid with storage and grid-stabilizing machinery. Others are becoming hubs with a comprehensive approach to green industrialization, including renewables, hydrogen, battery storage, and on-site manufacturing. Emerging technologies like long-duration batteries and advanced nuclear power are also being integrated into these sites.

Despite progress in shutting down carbon-emitting power stations, new fossil-fired plants continue to be built at alarming rates. In 2024, 44 GW of coal and nearly 52 GW of gas-fired plants were added globally, while just over 30 GW were decommissioned. Repurposing existing plants presents a viable solution to these challenges, leveraging existing infrastructure to accelerate the transition to clean energy.
